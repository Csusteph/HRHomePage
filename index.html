Our RCCB Objective

OGSM — Objectives, Goals, Strategies & Measures

The plan on a page — how we translate our People Vision into focused objectives, measurable goals, the strategies we'll execute, and the measures we'll track.

Attract
Goal
Bring in top, diverse talent that fits our culture and accelerates our wins.
Strategies
  • Strengthen employer brand & EVP across all channels
  • Deepen early-career, military & community pipelines
  • Modernize the candidate experience end-to-end
  • Build proactive sourcing for hard-to-fill roles
Measures
  • Time-to-fill
  • Quality of hire
  • Offer acceptance rate
  • Diversity of slate & hires
Develop
Goal
Grow capabilities and leaders that future-ready the business.
Strategies
  • Targeted leadership development at every level
  • Career pathways & internal mobility programs
  • Learning experiences tied to role & readiness
  • Coaching, mentoring & stretch assignments
Measures
  • % internal promotions
  • Succession pipeline depth
  • Training completion & effectiveness
  • Performance distribution
Cultivate
Goal
Build a winning, inclusive culture where teammates thrive.
Strategies
  • Listen & act on engagement insights
  • Recognize & reward winning behaviors
  • Strengthen safety & wellbeing programs
  • Activate inclusion through everyday actions
Measures
  • Engagement score
  • Voluntary turnover
  • Safety incident rate
  • Inclusion index
Be the Best
Goal
Deliver excellence in HR essentials and partner the business to win.
Strategies
  • Simplify & standardize core HR processes
  • Use workforce analytics to drive decisions
  • Embed continuous improvement in everything
  • Strengthen HR business partnership
Measures
  • HR service NPS
  • Process cycle time
  • Compliance & audit results
  • Productivity improvement
